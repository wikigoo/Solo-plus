بالا: کاشی بزرگ و رنگارنگ Toynbee که در مرکز شهر واشنگتن دی سی یافت شده است پایین: نمای نزدیک از زبانه پایینی آن، که ظاهراً به اتحاد جماهیر شوروی اشاره دارد ، که سال‌ها پیش از گرفته شدن این عکس از بین رفته بود. ("همانطور که رسانه‌ها می‌گویند، اتحاد جماهیر شوروی و جبهه‌ها مخالف آن هستند.")

کاشی‌های **توینبی که** **پلاک‌های توینبی** نیز نامیده می‌شوند ، پیام‌هایی با منشأ ناشناخته هستند که در [آسفالت](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asphalt_concrete "بتن آسفالتی") خیابان‌ها در حدود دو دوجین شهر بزرگ در ایالات متحده و سه شهر آمریکای جنوبی جاسازی شده‌اند. [^1] [^2] از دهه 1980، چند صد کاشی کشف شده است. آنها عموماً به اندازه یک [پلاک خودرو](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plate "پلاک ثبت وسیله نقلیه") آمریکایی هستند (تقریباً 30 در 15 سانتی‌متر یا 12 در 6 اینچ )، اما گاهی اوقات به طور قابل توجهی بزرگتر هستند. آنها حاوی نوعی از کتیبه زیر هستند:

> ایده توینبی  
> در فیلم «۲۰۰۱:  
> زنده کردن مردگان  
> در سیاره مشتری»

برخی از کاشی‌های پیچیده‌تر، حاوی بیانیه‌های سیاسی مرموز نیز هستند یا خوانندگان را به ساخت و نصب کاشی‌های مشابه ترغیب می‌کنند. در ابتدا جنس مواد مورد استفاده برای ساخت کاشی‌ها ناشناخته بود، اما شواهدی پدیدار شده است که نشان می‌دهد آنها ممکن است در درجه اول از لایه‌های مشمع [کف اتاق](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linoleum "مشمع کف اتاق") و ترکیب پرکننده ترک آسفالت ساخته شده باشند. [^3] مقالاتی در مورد این کاشی‌ها از اواسط دهه 1990 شروع به انتشار کردند، اگرچه ممکن است ارجاعات در اواسط دهه 1980 شروع شده باشد. [^4]

## تاریخچه

اولین مشاهده تایید شده کاشی‌های توینبی در سال 1983 در [فیلادلفیا](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia "فیلادلفیا") بود ، [^5] و اولین اشاره شناخته شده به آنها در رسانه‌ها در سال 1994 در *[روزنامه بالتیمور سان](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Baltimore_Sun "روزنامه بالتیمور سان")* صورت گرفت. [^6] [^7] نامه‌ای در سال 1983 به *[روزنامه فیلادلفیا اینکوایرر](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Philadelphia_Inquirer "روزنامه فیلادلفیا اینکوایرر")* به یک کمپین مستقر در فیلادلفیا با مضامین مشابه موارد ذکر شده در کاشی‌ها (مثلاً احیای مردگان در [مشتری](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jupiter "مشتری") ، [استنلی کوبریک](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Kubrick "استنلی کوبریک") و [آرنولد جی. توینبی](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnold_J._Toynbee "آرنولد جی. توینبی") ) اشاره کرد، اما به کاشی‌ها اشاره نکرد. [^8]

در ایالات متحده، کاشی‌ها رسماً تا غرب، [کانزاس سیتی، میسوری](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas_City,_Missouri "کانزاس سیتی، میزوری") ، [^1] تا شمال [، بوستون، ماساچوست](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston "بوستون") ، [^1] و تا جنوب، [ریچموند، ویرجینیا،](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond,_Virginia "ریچموند، ویرجینیا") مشاهده شده‌اند. [^9] از سال 2002، تعداد بسیار کمی کاشی جدید که به عنوان اثر هنرمند اصلی در نظر گرفته می‌شوند، خارج از منطقه نزدیک [فیلادلفیا، پنسیلوانیا](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia "فیلادلفیا") ظاهر شده‌اند ، اگرچه یک مورد قابل توجه در حومه [کنتیکت](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connecticut "کنتیکت") در سال 2006، [^10] و یکی در [ادیسون، نیوجرسی](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edison,_New_Jersey "ادیسون، نیوجرسی") در سال 2007 مشاهده شد. [^11] کاشی‌های کپی احتمالی در [نوبلزویل، ایندیانا](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noblesville,_Indiana "نوبلزویل، ایندیانا") ؛ [بوفالو، نیویورک](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffalo,_New_York "بوفالو، نیویورک") ؛ [سیراکیوز، نیویورک](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse,_New_York "سیراکیوز، نیویورک") ؛ سانفرانسیسکو، کالیفرنیا؛ [پورتلند، اورگان](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland,_Oregon "پورتلند، اورگان") ؛ و [راسول، نیومکزیکو](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roswell,_New_Mexico "راسول، نیومکزیکو") و همچنین در سال 1997 در [دیترویت، میشیگان](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit "دیترویت") و در سال 2013 در [تولسا، اوکلاهما](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulsa,_Oklahoma "تولسا، اکلاهما") مشاهده شده‌اند. [^12] [^13] بسیاری از کاشی‌های قدیمی‌تر که گمان می‌رود کار کاشی‌کار اصلی باشند، در اثر عبور و مرور فرسایش یافته‌اند، اما از سال 2011 کاشی‌های قدیمی‌تر در [پیتسبورگ، پنسیلوانیا](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pittsburgh "پیتسبورگ") ؛ [سنت لوئیس، میسوری](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Louis "سنت لوئیس") ؛ [سینسیناتی، اوهایو](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cincinnati "سینسیناتی") ، [کلیولند، اوهایو](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleveland "کلیولند") ؛ و آمریکای جنوبی، و سایر مکان‌ها، باقی مانده‌اند.

![کاشی توینبی در شهر نیویورک، ۲۰۱۳](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Toynbeetilenyc.jpg)

کاشی توینبی در شهر نیویورک، ۲۰۱۳

در 19 ژوئن 2013، کاشی‌هایی شبیه به کاشی‌های توینبی در خیابانی در [توپکا، کانزاس](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topeka,_Kansas "توپیکا، کانزاس") ظاهر شدند. آنها تا عصر روز بعد برداشته شدند. [^14] [کمتر از یک ماه بعد، در 17 ژوئیه 2013، کاشی‌ای شبیه به کاشی‌های توینبی در خیابانی در سالت لیک سیتی، یوتا](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_Lake_City "سالت لیک سیتی") ظاهر شد . [^15]

Newer tiles have been embedded on several major highways in Pennsylvania, including [Interstate 476](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_476 "بزرگراه بین ایالتی ۴۷۶") in [Delaware County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delaware_County,_Pennsylvania "شهرستان دلاور، پنسیلوانیا"), and on [Interstate 95](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_95_in_Pennsylvania "بزرگراه بین ایالتی ۹۵ در پنسیلوانیا"). About six more were found on [U.S. 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_1_in_Pennsylvania "جاده شماره ۱ ایالات متحده در پنسیلوانیا") northbound starting in [Drexel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drexel_Hill,_Pennsylvania "درکسل هیل، پنسیلوانیا") in [Delaware County, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delaware_County,_Pennsylvania "شهرستان دلاور، پنسیلوانیا") in 2007 and 2008. The plates are much larger than the originals and have red italic writing on them. In 2016, some tiles started re-appearing in Philadelphia.[^16]

## Interpretations

### People and things referred to

![](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Toynbee_tile_near_white_house_2002.jpg)

Commonly, a city will have a couple of large and colorful tiles along with numerous small and simple tiles like this one, just a block from the White House.

In a documentary film about the tiles, [Justin Duerr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Duerr "جاستین دوئر") assumes that "Toynbee" refers to the 20th century British historian [Arnold J. Toynbee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnold_J._Toynbee "آرنولد جی. توینبی"), and that "Kubrick's 2001" is a reference to the 1968 film *[2001: A Space Odyssey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001:_A_Space_Odyssey_\(film\) "۲۰۰۱: یک ادیسه فضایی (فیلم)")*, a film co-written and directed by filmmaker [Stanley Kubrick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Kubrick "استنلی کوبریک"), about a crewed mission to [Jupiter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jupiter "مشتری").[^17] The former speculation site toynbee.net [^12] theorized that 'Toynbee' referred to [Ray Bradbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Bradbury "ری بردبری") 's short story " [The Toynbee Convector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Toynbee_Convector "کنوکتور توینبی") ".[^18]

The majority of tiles contain text similar to that above, although a second set is often found nearby. Several of these allude to a mass [conspiracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conspiracy_theory "نظریه توطئه") between the press (including newspaper magnate [John S. Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_S._Knight "جان اس. نایت") of [Knight-Ridder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight-Ridder "شوالیه سوار")), the U.S. government, the USSR (including tiles seemingly made years after the Soviet Union's dissolution), and "hellion Jews".[^19]

A tile that used to be located in [Santiago de Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago_de_Chile "سانتیاگو دو شیلی") mentions a street address: 2624 S. 7th [Philadelphia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia "فیلادلفیا"), Pennsylvania. In 2006, the occupants of the house stated that they knew nothing about the tiles and were annoyed by people who asked,[^20] although the house was the former residence of a named recluse and alleged tile-maker, as shown in the 2011 documentary film *[Resurrect Dead: The Mystery of the Toynbee Tiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resurrect_Dead:_The_Mystery_of_the_Toynbee_Tiles "زنده کردن مردگان: راز کاشی‌های توینبی")*. Toynbee-tile enthusiasts believe that a native Philadelphian created the Toynbee tiles because of the large number that appear in the city, their apparent age, the variety of carving styles, the presence of the "tile creator's screed," and the Philadelphia address on the Santiago tile.

### Possible subjects

#### Arnold J. Toynbee's "The Idea"

According to letters written by the tiler, allegedly uncovered by Toynbee tile researchers in Philadelphia in 2006,[^21] "Toynbee's idea" stems from a passage in Arnold Toynbee's book *Experiences*:

> Human nature presents human minds with a puzzle which they have not yet solved and may never succeed in solving, for all that we can tell. The dichotomy of a human being into 'soul' and 'body' is not a datum of experience. No one has ever been, or ever met, a living human soul without a body... Someone who accepts—as I myself do, taking it on trust—the present-day scientific account of the Universe may find it impossible to believe that a living creature, once dead, can come to life again; but, if he did entertain this belief, he would be thinking more 'scientifically' if he thought in the Christian terms of a psychosomatic resurrection than if he thought in the shamanistic terms of a disembodied spirit.[^22]

#### Ray Bradbury's "The Toynbee Convector"

Another possible interpretation is that the Toynbee reference comes from the science fiction writer [Ray Bradbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Bradbury "ری بردبری") 's short story " [The Toynbee Convector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Toynbee_Convector "کنوکتور توینبی") ",[^18] which alludes to Toynbee's idea that to survive, humankind must always rush to meet the future and believe in a better world, and must always aim far beyond what is practically possible, to achieve something barely within reach. Thus the message might be that humanity ought to strive to colonize [Jupiter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jupiter "مشتری") —as in Clarke's work—or something greater, to survive.

#### Arthur C. Clarke's "Jupiter V"

[Arthur C. Clarke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_C._Clarke "آرتور سی. کلارک") 's short story " [Jupiter V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jupiter_Five "مشتری پنج") " involves a space ship named the *[Arnold Toynbee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnold_J._Toynbee "آرنولد جی. توینبی")* on a mission to Jupiter. It contains many ideas and concepts Clarke would later reuse when writing *2001*.[^23] [^24]

#### David Mamet's "4 A.M."

Playwright [David Mamet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Mamet "دیوید ممت") has spoken of his belief that the tiles are an homage to one of his plays, and has described it as "the weirdest thing that ever happened". In his 1983 work "4 A.M." (published in the collection *Goldberg Street: Short Plays and Monologues* in 1985), a radio host based on [Larry King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_King "لری کینگ") impatiently listens to a caller who contends that the movie *2001*, based on the writings of Arnold Toynbee, speaks of the plan to reconstitute life on Jupiter. The radio show host quickly points out the factual errors in the caller's assertion and the logical fallacies of his plan.[^25]

Researchers for the 2011 documentary *Resurrect Dead: The Mystery of the Toynbee Tiles* claim to have uncovered several pieces of evidence that predate Mamet's play, including an actual 1980 call by the tiler to Larry King's radio show.[^26] They cite a 1983 article in *The Philadelphia Inquirer* which mentions a local man "contacting talk shows and newspapers to spread the message" about bringing the dead to life on Jupiter, as depicted in the film *2001*.[^27]

## Potential creators

![](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Toynbee-1.jpg) ![](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Toynbee-2.jpg) ![](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Toynbee-3.jpg)

Three tiles placed on the [Avenue of the Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avenue_of_the_Arts_\(Philadelphia\) "خیابان هنرها (فیلادلفیا)") section of [Broad Street in Philadelphia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broad_Street_\(Philadelphia\) "خیابان براد (فیلادلفیا)"). The tiles are made of linoleum cemented onto normal-sized paving bricks.

In 1983, a man identifying himself as a social worker named James Morasco contacted talk shows and newspapers with his theory of colonizing Jupiter with the dead inhabitants of Earth, claiming to have come across the idea while reading a book by historian Arnold Toynbee.[^8] In a conversation with *[The Philadelphia Inquirer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Philadelphia_Inquirer "روزنامه فیلادلفیا اینکوایرر")*, Morasco discussed how Toynbee's book contained a theory on bringing dead molecules back to life and that this was later depicted in the movie *2001: A Space Odyssey*.[^8] The caller had founded what the *Inquirer* called a "Jupiter colonization organization", known as the Minority Association.[^8]

In 1996, the *[Kansas City Star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas_City_Star "کانزاس سیتی استار")* editor [Doug Worgul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doug_Worgul "داگ ورگول") discovered a "Toynbee Tile" at the corner of 13th and Grand in downtown Kansas City. Investigating the story seven years later, he found that the tile was still there, and he determined that the street had last been resurfaced in 1996. Comparing the tile to those in other cities, a local police detective felt that "clearly it was created by the same hand" and concluded that despite referring to a "movement", the creator was acting alone.[^19]

In 2003, Worgul called the only James Morasco in the Philadelphia telephone book and was told by the man's wife that her husband had died in March that year, aged 88. When asked about the tiles, Morasco's widow said that her husband "didn't know anything about it." [^19] Worgul doubted that this was the tile maker.[^19] [Action News Philadelphia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_News_Philadelphia "اخبار اکشن فیلادلفیا") spoke to the widow of a man named James Joseph Morasco and identified him as a Philadelphia carpenter who had died in 2003, aged 87. His wife did not recognise the tiles and said her husband had had no interest in Jupiter.[^20] If Morasco had died at either age in 2003, he would have been in his seventies when most of the tiles were laid.[^20]

In the 2011 documentary *[Resurrect Dead: The Mystery of the Toynbee Tiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resurrect_Dead:_The_Mystery_of_the_Toynbee_Tiles "زنده کردن مردگان: راز کاشی‌های توینبی")*, artist and Toynbee Tile enthusiast [Justin Duerr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Duerr "جاستین دوئر") said that he considered the tiles to be the work of a single person and attributed them to the reclusive Philadelphia resident Severino "Sevy" Verna.[^28] Duerr believed Verna used the name "James Morasco" as an alias.[^28] The streets surrounding Verna's residence were littered with small "proto-tiles" that Duerr believed were tests, and [ham radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ham_radio "رادیو ژامبون") enthusiasts reported Verna might have broadcast a message via [short wave radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short_wave_radio "رادیو موج کوتاه") about his theories.[^28] Based on comments from Verna's neighbors about him driving a car without a passenger seat, Duerr suspected Verna placed the tiles through a hole in the floor of his car.

New tiles have been seen in Philadelphia since 2003. Between 2002 and 2007, many such tiles displayed a different font and styling than the older tiles and tended to leave out words that were found on the originals: "raise" is often substituted for "resurrect," and prepositions are frequently omitted. Beginning in 2007, tiles were discovered in Philadelphia that are quite similar to the original tiles, leading some to believe that everything has been the work of the same person throughout the life of the tile phenomenon. The font and message are the same as the old ones, and the subtext is a return to some of the older ideas as well. These tiles were glued with a thicker layer of asphalt glue or sealant than older ones.

## Usage

### Deployment

Toynbee-tile enthusiast [Justin Duerr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Duerr "جاستین دوئر") claims to have once found and examined a newly installed tile. This new tile was wrapped in [tar paper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tar_paper "کاغذ قیر اندود") and placed on a busy street early in the morning. From this find and other evidence, Duerr believes that the pressure exerted by cars driving over the tile for weeks on end pushes the tile into the road surface. Eventually, the tar paper wears away, exposing the message.

### Destruction, conservation, and public acknowledgment

Tiles that are located in the middle of busy streets and near traffic exchanges tend to quickly disintegrate due to normal traffic flow and road repair. In contrast, smaller tiles and those located closer to pedestrian crosswalks tend to be in better condition.

Hundreds of tiles have been destroyed during the course of regular road maintenance.[^29] The city of Chicago has declared the tiles "vandalism" and removes any tiles that it finds, considering them to be "no different than graffiti." [^18]

A large tile complex, the tile maker's apparent rant against his enemies, was destroyed when Chestnut Street in Philadelphia was repaved.[^30] One tile has been damaged and unreadable since 1996. It is located at the corners of Talcahuano and Santa Fé streets in [Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buenos_Aires "بوئنوس آیرس"), Argentina.

There is no public or private agency dedicated to conserving Toynbee tiles. Many tiles now exist only as photographs taken before their destruction. The tiles have enjoyed attention from American and European media outlets, including from *[The New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times "نیویورک تایمز")*, *[The Chicago Sun-Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Chicago_Sun-Times "شیکاگو سان-تایمز")*, *[Spiegel Online](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spiegel_Online "اشپیگل آنلاین")*, and [NPR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NPR "ان پی آر"). In 2011, Philadelphia-based filmmakers [Justin Duerr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Duerr "جاستین دوئر"), Jon Foy, Colin Smith, and Steve Weinik released *Resurrect Dead: The Mystery of the Toynbee Tiles*, an independent documentary film about the tiles.[^31] The film was selected for the 2011 [Sundance Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundance_Film_Festival "جشنواره فیلم ساندنس") in the U.S. Documentary category, and Foy won the category's Directing Award.[^32]

As of October 2015, the Streets Department of Philadelphia recognizes Toynbee Tiles as [street art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Street_art "هنر خیابانی"), and "will save one or two of the Toynbee Tiles only if there is a fast and affordable method for removing them." [^33]
